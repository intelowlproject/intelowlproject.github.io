Almost four years have passed since the GreedyBear launch in 2021. Much has changed since then, and some of the underlying technologies require an update. That's why we are releasing a new major version of GreedyBear which comes with the most current versions of Django (5.2) and PostgreSQL (18). These changes will ensure our project remains greedy and up-to-date for years to come but require some manual intervention. You can find a detailed upgrade guide [here](https://intelowlproject.github.io/docs/GreedyBear/UpgradeToV2/).
